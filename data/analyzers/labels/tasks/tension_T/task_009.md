1. KIRSTEN BLOMQVIST, forties, sits at the stern, wrapped in a blanket, eyes fixed on a fixed point in the mist. / RAJESH IYER, fifties, her husband, sits at the bow with a thermos between his knees. / They have not spoken in an hour.
2. He unscrews the cap. / He pours half into the lid, passes it back along the boat without a word. / She takes it. / She drinks. / She returns it.
3. He sets the lid on the thwart. / He studies the water.
4. RAJESH: Did you hear something? / KIRSTEN: No. / RAJESH: There was a bell. / KIRSTEN: There's no bell.
5. He listens. / The fog presses in. / She pulls the blanket tighter and looks at his hands, which are shaking, and then at her own, which are not.
6. She reaches under the seat. / She finds the second thermos. / She sets it on the bench between them, unopened. / RAJESH: We'll want that later. / KIRSTEN: Yes.
7. He nods. / She nods. / They both look out at the gray.
8. Far off, faint, one clear note. / A bell.
9. Neither of them turns.

2. A hiss. / A younger woman's voice, warm and slightly breathless, fills the booth. / ODALYS: Test, test. Okay. This is Odalys, and it's the first of June, and the little ones are asleep, so I will whisper the schedule.
3. Odalys closes her eyes. / Her hand drifts across the counter and rests on a brass stamp. / ODALYS: Seven a.m., the fishermen. Eight, the schoolchildren, mind the puddles. Noon, nobody. Nobody ever, and that's when I read.
4. The tape hisses. / Outside, a gull drops onto the pier rail and watches her.
5. ODALYS: If anyone ever hears this, I want you to know it was a good job. The best view in the county, and nobody to talk to but the tide.
6. Odalys smiles, then stops smiling.
7. She slides the stamp into her cardigan pocket, switches off the lamp, and presses STOP. / The silence rings.
8. ODALYS: It was.
9. She pulls the shutter down.
